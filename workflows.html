The practical playbook for building your first repeatable AI-powered workflow. By the end, you will have one you've run three times.

What this playbook is

In v1 you shipped something once. This playbook is about work that repeats. You design the steps once, AI drafts the output, you check it, and a free scheduling tool publishes or sends it at the time you choose.

There are two paths. Share what you built, with a weekly update or challenge post for a game, or regular posts for a website. Or build a personal workflow: a weekly meal plan and shopping list, a family week-ahead email, or tailored job applications. You don't need users, customers or feedback to start.

You don't need to have read v1, though if you did, you'll reuse what you built. Every chapter ends with a 15-minute exercise, and the tools are free.

What is guaranteed: you will learn, and you will learn fast.

By the end of this playbook, you will

01 Spot what repeats. Know which tasks are worth turning into a workflow, and which aren't.
02 Map it before you build it. The five parts of every workflow, mapped with AI asking you the questions.
03 Build it once. A standing brief, a reusable prompt, and a Project in Claude to keep them in.
04 Let it run. Your output scheduled to publish or send while you're doing something else.
05 Run it again. Three runs, each faster than the last, with quality you've checked.
